Smart Traffic Light
Automation System

An integrated hardware and software solution combining a centralized web dashboard, mobile app, and ESP32 microcontrollers to manage physical traffic lights, emergency priorities, and incident reporting in real-time.

Core System Capabilities

📡

Hardware Integration

Direct control of 220V physical traffic lights via ESP32 and relay modules.

🚨

Emergency Priority

Traffic Police can instantly override signals for approaching ambulances or fire trucks.

📝

Incident Reporting

Officers can log accidents, violations, and hardware issues directly from the field.

📊

Centralized Control

Administrators can configure timings, manage users, and generate PDF/Excel reports.
